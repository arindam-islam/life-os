# GOAL-002: Revenue Generation

Last Updated: 24 August 2026

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goal Status

Status:

ACTIVE

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision

Build sustainable income streams that create financial growth and
long-term independence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goal

Create validated revenue opportunities through:

-   Automation services
-   AI workflow solutions
-   Content systems
-   Digital products
-   Other validated opportunities

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Target

Primary target:

Generate ₹15 Lakhs net revenue by 31 December 2026.

Revenue growth should follow measurable stages:

\$1 → \$10 → \$100 → \$1K → \$10K → ₹15L+

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goal Type

Growth Target

Completion rule:

Revenue generation is a continuous growth goal.

When a target is achieved: - Evaluate the next growth stage - Increase
the target if the opportunity exists - Define the next milestone

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Indicators

Progress is measured by:

-   First revenue generated
-   Customer validation
-   Repeatable revenue process
-   Revenue growth
-   Paying customers acquired
-   Retention and repeatability

------------------------------------------------------------------------

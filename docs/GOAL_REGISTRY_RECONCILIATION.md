# Life OS Goal Registry Reconciliation

Last Updated: 23 August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document records the migration from the historical Life OS goal registry
to the strategic outcome-based goal model.

The purpose:

- Remove duplicate goal identities
- Separate strategic goals from engineering experiments
- Maintain a single source of truth
- Prevent AI systems from acting on conflicting goal definitions

---

# Previous Goal Registry

Historical goals contained a mixture of:

- Business outcomes
- Technical experiments
- Infrastructure tasks
- Prototype initiatives

Example:

GOAL-003 previously represented:

Mobile Slack Command Center & Thread Workforce

This was an engineering experiment, not a primary life/business outcome.

---

# Reclassified Engineering Components

The following items are not strategic goals.

They should be managed as engineering projects/components.

Examples:

- Mobile Slack Command Center
- Multimodal Ingestion Pipeline
- Bridge Webhook Testing

Reason:

They support Life OS but are not the final outcomes Life OS exists to achieve.

---

# Registry Rule

A GOAL ID must represent:

- A meaningful outcome
- A measurable target
- A strategic direction

A GOAL ID should not represent:

- A script
- A feature
- A prototype
- A technical experiment

---

# Final Principle

Life OS should optimize for outcomes.

Technology exists to support goals, not replace them.

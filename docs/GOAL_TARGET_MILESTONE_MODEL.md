---

# Hierarchy Model

## Level 1: Vision

Definition:

A long-term desired direction or life outcome.

Examples:

- Financial independence
- Career growth
- Building valuable businesses
- Personal development

A vision usually does not have a completion state.

It evolves over time.

---

# Level 2: Goal

Definition:

A meaningful objective that moves toward a vision.

A goal answers:

"What major outcome are we trying to achieve?"

Examples:

## Goal:

Build a scalable faceless content business.

## Goal:

Secure a strong Product / Technical Operations role.

---

# Level 3: Target

Definition:

A measurable outcome attached to a goal.

A target answers:

"How will we know the goal is progressing or achieved?"

Targets should include:

- Metric
- Desired value
- Timeline (when applicable)

---

# Target Types

## Achievement Target

A target that completes once achieved.

Example:

Goal:

Secure a Product Operations role.

Target:

Receive an offer between ₹16.5 LPA and ₹20 LPA.

Completion:

Offer accepted within target range.

---

## Growth Target

A target that represents continuous expansion.

Example:

Goal:

Build a faceless content business.

Target:

Generate $100K/month revenue.

Completion:

Target achieved.

Next decision:

- Maintain current level
- Increase target
- Create a new growth stage

---

# Level 4: Milestones

Definition:

Intermediate checkpoints that show progress toward a target.

Milestones prevent large goals from becoming unrealistic.

---

# Example: Faceless Content Business

Vision:

Build financial independence.

↓

Goal:

Build a scalable faceless content business.

↓

Target:

Reach $100K/month revenue.

↓

Milestones:

Stage 1:

Build content system.

Success indicators:

- Research workflow exists
- Content strategy process exists
- Production workflow validated

---

Stage 2:

First audience validation.

Success indicators:

- Videos published
- Initial views generated
- Audience response measured

---

Stage 3:

Revenue validation.

Success indicators:

- First $1 earned
- Monetization process validated

---

Stage 4:

Growth stages.

Progression:

$10/month

↓

$100/month

↓

$1K/month

↓

$10K/month

↓

$100K/month

---

After target achievement:

Life OS evaluates:

Should the target:

- Continue?
- Increase?
- Change direction?

Example:

$100K/month achieved.

New possible target:

$1M/month revenue.

↓

Receive an offer between ₹16.5 LPA and ₹20 LPA.

↓

Applications submitted.

Recruiter conversations started.

Interviews completed.

↓

---

Completion Rule:

Achievement-based goals are completed when the target is achieved.

Future improvement should create a new goal or revise the target.

---

# Goal Lifecycle

A goal can have these states:

## ACTIVE

Currently being worked on.

---

## BLOCKED

Progress stopped due to a dependency.

---

## COMPLETED

Target achieved.

---

## EVOLVED

Original target achieved and a new target defined.

---

## ARCHIVED

No longer relevant.

---

# Decision Rules

Before executing actions, Life OS should ask:

1. Which goal does this support?
2. What target does this move forward?
3. Which milestone does this achieve?
4. How will success be measured?

---

# Progress Measurement

Progress is not measured by:

- Number of tasks completed
- Amount of code written
- Number of tools created

Progress is measured by:

- Real outcomes achieved
- Targets reached
- Revenue generated
- Career improvement
- Time saved
- Better decisions

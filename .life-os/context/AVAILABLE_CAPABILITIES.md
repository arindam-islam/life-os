# Purpose

This document defines the capabilities currently available inside Life OS.

The objective is to maintain a truthful understanding of:

- What exists
- What works
- What is planned
- What requires development

---

# Capability Status Model

Each capability is classified as:

VERIFIED

- Exists
- Tested
- Reliable

PARTIAL

- Exists
- Requires improvement or validation

PLANNED

- Designed but not implemented

EXPERIMENTAL

- Prototype or exploration
